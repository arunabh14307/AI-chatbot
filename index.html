Answers to Common Student Queries

Find quick answers verified against university regulations and standard operating procedures.

As per Lovely Professional University academic regulations, students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each individual course to be eligible to sit for the End Term Examinations.

Note: In case of documented medical emergencies or official university representation, attendance duty leaves may be processed according to official university guidelines submitted through the portal.

Hostel resident students must submit their leave request digitally through the UMS / Mobile App Hostel Module:

  1. Log in to your student portal account.
  2. Navigate to the Hostel Management section and select Apply Leave / Outpass.
  3. Select the leave category (Local Outpass, Day Pass, or Home Leave) and specify dates.
  4. Home leaves typically trigger automated parental verification via SMS/call depending on the student's program and year.
  5. Once approved by the warden, the digital outpass barcode can be verified at university exit turnstiles.

Continuous Assessment (CA) evaluates a student's performance throughout the semester through multiple components, which may include:

  • Class quizzes and objective tests
  • Assignments and coding exercises
  • Presentations, mini-projects, or lab evaluations
  • Class participation and attendance credits

The consolidated best marks out of the allocated tasks are finalized by faculty members and posted on your UMS grade sheet before term-end examinations.

If you forgot your password or cannot access UMS:

  1. Click the Forgot Password link on the official UMS login page.
  2. Enter your registered Student Registration Number.
  3. An OTP (One-Time Password) will be dispatched to your registered mobile number and official university email.
  4. Verify the OTP and create a strong new password.

If your registered mobile number has changed, visit the University IT Services helpdesk with your physical student ID card to update your credentials.

Fee statements and receipts are accessible on UMS under Fee Management > Fee Statement / Online Payment. From this dashboard, you can view tuition fees, hostel installments, examination fees, and download stamped digital tax/tuition payment receipts for educational loan verification.

RMS is Lovely Professional University's centralized single-window grievance and query redressal platform. Students can log requests for academic issues, hostel queries, mess feedback, or administrative certificates. Each ticket is assigned a unique tracking number with a defined resolution timeframe.

About the Project

About LPU Assist

A student-centric support portal built to streamline information access and empower Lovely Professional University students.

Our Mission

Navigating university systems, examination criteria, residential rules, and academic guidelines should be smooth and immediate. LPU Assist brings student-relevant resources into a unified, clean, and intuitive conversational interface.

Core Principles

  • No Fabricated Information: We adhere strictly to verified academic circulars and standard university operating procedures.
  • Responsive & Accessible: Built for flawless usability on smartphones, tablets, and laptops.
  • Privacy Conscious: No personal credentials or passwords are ever stored or requested by the assistant.

Useful University Portals

UMS (University Management System) Class attendance, continuous assessments, and fee management.
RMS (Relationship Management System) Official student grievance redressal and departmental queries.
Central Library Services E-resources, reference catalogs, and study hall facilities.
